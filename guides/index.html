The Provence Wine Library

A clear, independent starting point for Provence wine in Australia—from the map and the grapes to the glass and the table.

Start here: If you are new to Provence wine, begin with the Australia guide. Use the appellation comparison to understand place, the grape guide to understand the blend, and the glossary to decode a label. For the bottle in front of you, use the tasting and pairing guides.

Provence is more than one pale style of rosé. It is a Mediterranean wine region with coastal and inland appellations, diverse soils, distinctive grapes, and serious rosé, red and white wines. This library brings the site’s guides into one path so people—and search or answer engines—can find the right source without guessing.
